Battle Bots — Art Direction Study

Live, unscripted. Bots are actually colliding and damaging each other.

Machined bodiesGradient metal, panel seams, corner bolts, specular streak, and a cast shadow that implies real height off the floor.
Weapon motion blurSpinners render as a swept gradient arc, not a drawn blade — reads as speed at a glance.
Impact languageSpark burst, expanding shockwave ring, white damage flash, and knockback proportional to real impact velocity.
Floor scarringTire scuffs, scrape marks, and scorch craters accumulate permanently. The arena tells the story of the fight.
Health arcsRing around each bot, with a pale ghost bar that drains a beat behind — so you feel the size of a hit.
Your bot glowsThe member watching gets a pulsing ring and name tag on their own machine. Render-only, never affects the result.
